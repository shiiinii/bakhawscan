ABOUT THE PROJECT

Protecting mangroves starts with observation.

BakhawScan is a student research prototype designed to support documentation of visible leaf symptoms in mangrove monitoring. It does not identify exact diseases or classify mangrove species.

Three symptom classes

Visible patterns the model is intended to locate

Black spots

Dark or black marks appearing on the leaf surface.

BLACK SPOTS

Brown spots

Brown discoloration or visible brown patches on leaf tissue.

BROWN SPOTS

White spots

Pale or white marks that can be seen in the leaf image.

WHITE SPOTS

Four mangrove species included in the proposal

The proposed dataset includes these species, but the app does not automatically identify which species is pictured.

Lumnitzera littoreaRhizophora apiculataScyphiphora hydrophyllaceaSonneratia alba

What the findings mean

Spotting can have multiple causes. A single leaf photo may not reveal whether a mark is linked to disease, stress, physical damage, or another factor. Use detections to guide documentation, not to claim a confirmed diagnosis.

Research and learning support — not a replacement for expert assessment.

Your local data

Understand what is stored on this device

The app can save up to 12 recent scan previews, detection coordinates, and timestamps in this browser's local storage. This history isn't shared between devices. Clearing browser data can delete it.

API response format

The frontend keeps the original API contract and expects pixel coordinates relative to the uploaded image:

{
  "image_width": 1280,
  "image_height": 960,
  "detections": [
    {"class_name": "Brown_spot", "confidence": 0.92,
     "box": [120, 85, 180, 145]}
  ]
}

Expected labels: black, brown, and white spots. The frontend also accepts plural/underscore variations.
